Solar Layout Planner

Give it the roof and the size you want. It lays out panels, strings, wiring, ballast and cost.

Planning-grade tool. Inverter data, city wind speeds, wind coefficients and prices are typical-class values: replace them with the datasheet of the equipment you buy and the signed calculation from your structure supplier and structural engineer. It does not replace a site survey, an IS 875-3 wind design, net-metering approval or the electrical inspector.
